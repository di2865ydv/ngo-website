Our Story

Community-Led,
Impact-Driven

Founded in 2012 by a group of grassroots social workers, RootBridge was born from a simple belief: that lasting change only happens when communities lead their own transformation. We work as facilitators, not saviours.

  • 🤝
    Community Ownership — Programs are co-designed with local leaders and beneficiaries to ensure cultural alignment and long-term sustainability.
  • 📊
    Evidence-Based Action — Every initiative is measured, evaluated, and refined using real-world data and community feedback loops.
  • 🌍
    Systemic Change — We address root causes — not symptoms — by engaging policy, infrastructure, and behaviour together.
What We Do

Six Pillars of
Transformation

Each program is deeply localised, measurable, and built to outlast our direct involvement — creating communities that thrive independently.

📚
Quality Education

Running 24 learning centres in rural areas, offering remedial classes, digital literacy, and scholarship support to children and first-generation learners.

8,200 students
🏥
Rural Health

Mobile health clinics, maternal care programs, and community health worker training to bridge the last-mile healthcare gap in underserved areas.

30+ villages
💼
Livelihood Skills

Vocational training, micro-enterprise support, and financial literacy workshops that create sustainable income for marginalised youth and women.

3,400 trained
🌾
Sustainable Farming

Promoting regenerative agriculture, water conservation, and organic farming techniques to improve food security and farmer income.

1,100 farmers
♀️
Women Empowerment

Self-help groups, legal aid, leadership training, and safe-space dialogues that transform gender dynamics at the village level.

210 SHGs
🧒
Child Protection

Child rights education, anti-trafficking awareness, and psychosocial support for vulnerable children in high-risk districts.

12 districts
